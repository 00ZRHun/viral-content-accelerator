The accelerator path

From idea to impact in five simple steps.

You don't need a new idea every day. You need a small number of repeatable moves you can run every week — with a coach in your corner when a post doesn't land.

  1. 01

    Research

    Find a trend or proven format that fits your niche. Start with what's already working.

    10 min
  2. 02

    Adapt

    Rewrite the hook and beats around your audience. Structure is the skeleton; your take is the story.

    15 min
  3. 03

    Create

    Film on the phone you already have. Keep the edit tight and let the idea do the work.

    45 min
  4. 04

    Publish

    Get a quick coach note, then post natively to the platform where your audience actually is.

    Same day
  5. 05

    Grow

    Read retention and comments. Repeat the format that landed, retire what didn't, and go again.

    Next week
The loop compounds.Each week gives you a better starting point than the last.

Who is the Viral Content Accelerator for?

Creators, founders, and social media teams who want a repeatable short-form system. It works in any niche and at any audience size — members have used it in fitness, cooking, finance, real estate, tattoo art, education, and plenty of others.

Do I need an existing audience?

No. The program includes onboarding, a seven-day sprint, and a library of proven formats built to give you a starting point even if you're at zero. Some member stories began with brand-new accounts.

What exactly is included?

Four months of live coaching, with at least eight live calls each month: weekly Format Coaching Calls plus optional Office Hours. You also get monthly 90-minute Monetization Workshops, expert video reviews in the community, 24/7 member chat, Format Finder™ with AI tools, all nine One Peak Creative courses, and replays of every call.

Which platforms does the program cover?

TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook. The formats are built around structure and human attention, not a single platform, so the same idea can be adapted wherever your audience spends time.

What is the 1,000,000-view guarantee?

If you post at least once a week, attend at least half of the calls, and don't cross 1,000,000 views within four months of joining, you can request a full refund. The current terms are confirmed at checkout. The guarantee rewards participation because the system needs reps to work.

How much does the program cost?

$5,999 paid in full, or $1,650 per month for four months ($6,600 total). Both options unlock the same coaching, tools, community, and guarantee. Current pricing and payment terms are confirmed on the official enrollment page.

How much time should I plan for?

Plan for one live call and at least one published video each week. Office Hours and replays make it possible to fit the program around a business or a day job, but the members who get the most out of it are the ones who post consistently.

How do I join?

Choose a payment option in the pricing section. You'll finish enrollment on One Peak Creative's official program page, and receive access to the community and onboarding after checkout.

Can I pause or cancel?

VCA is a four-month coaching commitment. If life gets in the way, you can pause access for 30 days. The 1,000,000-view guarantee is the safety net if the program doesn't deliver the result for you.
